Our Signature Framework

SB-RCOM: The Small Business Risk & Compliance Journey

A lightweight, anticipatory, audit-ready operating system.

Built specifically for small businesses — replacing scattered spreadsheets and one-off scrambles with a single, predictable rhythm of visibility, control, and review.

01

Visible Risk, Score Risks

Capture risks consistently using simple, repeatable forms and logs.

02

Design Controls

Score risks against a structured matrix to determine what actually matters most.

03

Track Compliance

Track obligations and deadlines in a single calendar instead of scattered reminders.

04

Focus Governance

Run monthly and quarterly reviews that keep the whole system alive and evolving.

05

Governance & Continuous Improvement

Maintain calendars, checklists, and audit-ready folders for predictable, repeatable compliance.
