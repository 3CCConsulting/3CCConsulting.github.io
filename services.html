Our Signature Framework

SB-RCOM: The Small Business Risk & Compliance Journey

A lightweight, anticipatory, audit-ready operating system.

Built specifically for small businesses — replacing scattered spreadsheets and one-off scrambles with a single, predictable rhythm of visibility, control, and review.

Small businesses run fast — but risk runs faster. SB-RCOM gives you a simple, powerful way to stay ahead of issues before they become expensive problems.

What SB-RCOM Solves

Click any one to see what handles it. Every answer names the actual tool — because a system you cannot point at is a promise, not a system.

A prequalification packet you can't complete
EC-01 Evidence Control

The packet asks for insurance, training records, corrective actions, and a quality manual. EC-01 is the register that says what evidence exists, where it lives, and who owns it — so the answer is a lookup instead of a weekend.

No quality manual
The documented system itself

SB-RCOM is the manual, written as the way you actually work rather than as a document that describes a company you don't run. It comes out of the tools, not out of a template.

A manual nobody follows
RIF-01 Risk Intake Form → RL-01 Risk Log

Systems get ignored when using them is extra work. Intake is one short form tied to a real job, and it feeds the log automatically. Following it becomes easier than not following it.

Certifications about to lapse
CC-01 Compliance Calendar

Every licence, certification, insurance renewal, and filing on one calendar with an owner and a lead time. You stop finding out a welder's cert expired when the customer's auditor finds it.

Findings from the last audit
MC-01 Mitigation & Control Plan

Each finding gets an owner, a control, a due date, and evidence that it closed. When the auditor comes back, you can show what changed instead of promising it did.

And while we are in there — the things that were wearing on you before anyone asked for paperwork.

Records scattered across trucks, binders, and phones
EC-01 Evidence Control

One register, one current version, one place to look. Most shops are not missing records — they have them in eleven places and no way to prove which one is current.

Missed deadlines
CC-01 Compliance Calendar → MRR-01 Monthly Risk Review

Deadlines get missed when nobody owns the calendar. The monthly review is a thirty-minute rhythm that surfaces what is coming before it is late.

Vendor and subcontractor quality
RIF-01 Risk Intake Form → RAM-01 Risk Assessment Matrix

Your subs' work becomes your work in the customer's eyes. Score them the same way you score everything else, so the risky ones are visible before they are on your job.

Owner burnout
OPD-01 Dashboard → QCR-01 Quarterly Compliance Review

Burnout here is a symptom of everything living in one head. The dashboard makes status visible without asking you, and the quarterly review is the one meeting that keeps it that way.
